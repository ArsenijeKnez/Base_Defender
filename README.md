## Base Defender

You can find a playable download of [Base Defender on itch.io](https://arsenijeknez.itch.io/base-defender).

Gather resources, recruit new people, and expand your base. Enemies attack at night, so prepare by building defenses and recruiting soldiers.

---

## Controls

- **A / D Keys** & **Mouse Scroll** — Camera movement
- **Left Mouse Button** — NPC selection & assignment
- **Right Mouse Button** — View building health & information
- **Esc Key** — Pause menu
- **I Key** — Information menu
- **1–8 Keys** — Building selection

---

## Gameplay

- You start with two workers and the main camp.
- Gather resources by selecting a worker and then selecting a resource.
- Recruit new people by clicking on the main camp and using its menu.
- Build new structures by recruiting and selecting a builder.
- When a builder is selected, available building locations will be highlighted in green.
- Use the buttons in the bottom-right corner of the screen to fast-forward time.
- Move soldiers by selecting them and then selecting the building you want them to defend.
